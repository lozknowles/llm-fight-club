VOICE CLONING CONSENT

This feature creates synthetic speech that can sound like the person whose voice is recorded.

Only enrol your own voice, or a voice whose speaker has explicitly given permission to create and use a synthetic version. The resulting voice may say words the speaker never recorded. It must not be treated as evidence that the real person said, approved or authorised those words.

Recordings and reusable profiles are sensitive personal material. They are stored privately for internal Fight Club announcement/commentary. You can delete them. They are not placed in public Git.

OmniVoice uses one accepted reference, selected deterministically nearest eight seconds. These varied readings explore delivery; they do not train model weights or guarantee every style in the generated voice.
